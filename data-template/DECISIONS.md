# Trip decisions and working notes

Keep this current. When a decision changes, edit the entry and add a line to the log at the bottom.

## Traveller and preferences
- (Who is going, passports and visa limits, how you like to travel, things to avoid.)

## Constraints
- (Fixed dates, budget, the number of days you have, things that are already booked.)

## Route decisions
- (Why the route looks the way it does. What you ruled out and why.)

## Verified transport facts
- (Times, prices and sources you have checked. Put the date you checked next to each.)

## Open questions
- (What still needs deciding or checking.)

## Log
- (Date: what changed and why.)
